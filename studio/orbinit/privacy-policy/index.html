About this policy

This policy covers Orbinit and its support website, published by DevMilanIan. You do not need to enter your name, email address, or a password to play. The game uses local saves, Firebase for shared Daily replays, Google AdMob for ads, and Apple for purchases.

Information on your device

Orbinit saves your progress, stars, scores, orbs, cosmetics, settings, and ad timing information on your device. Your device can also hold replays waiting to upload and the sign-in state used by Firebase.

If you choose Tilt controls, raw motion readings are processed on your device to steer the orb. The resulting gameplay controls can appear in shared Daily replays. Joystick controls are available without motion access.

Shared Daily replays

Daily replay sharing is enabled by default. When it is on, Daily attempts can be uploaded to Firebase and viewed publicly in Global Replay, including completed runs, falls, restarts, quits, and abandoned attempts.

Shared replay data includes a random run identifier, the Daily course and date, your equipped skin, steering and boosts, cube breaks, timing, and the result. Published replays are publicly readable and do not display your name or email address.

Firebase creates a persistent anonymous account identifier for uploads. Some replay ownership records are privately linked to that identifier. Firebase also processes technical information, such as IP addresses and user-agent information, to operate and protect its services.

Advertising and privacy choices

The iPhone app requests non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. Google may process device or app identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, and prevent fraud. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing.

Google's consent tools check whether ads may be requested. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy options to review or change your choices. Learn how Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Apple handles payments. Orbinit receives purchase and transaction information to verify purchases, unlock content, and restore purchases. The game does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Apple handles refund requests at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.

Your choices and data retention

Turn off Share Daily replays in Settings to stop future sharing and clear replays still queued on your device. A request already sent may finish. Turning sharing off does not remove replays already published.

Reset local progress resets gameplay progress and gameplay settings. It keeps your replay-sharing choice and ad timing information. It does not delete published replays, your anonymous Firebase account, or Apple's purchase records.

Published replays have no automatic expiry configured by the game and remain stored until removed. The app does not currently have a control to delete published replays or the anonymous Firebase account. Use the private contact on the Support page for questions about stored information.

Firebase retains authentication information until deletion is initiated; its security logs and service backups follow Firebase's retention practices. Google and Apple also retain service information under their own policies. You can read those policies in the links below.

This website and support

These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or a contact-form backend. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Providers, contact, and updates

For help with the game, a shared replay, or privacy questions, email mungoman775@gmail.com or use the Support website. This policy may change when the app changes; the date above identifies this version.
