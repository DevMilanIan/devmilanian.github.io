Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Orbinit → Settings → Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Allow the App Store time to connect, then try again if it is temporarily unavailable.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles purchase refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow Apple’s refund process.

How do I stop sharing Daily replays?

Open Settings and turn off Share Daily replays. This stops future sharing and clears replays waiting to upload. It does not remove runs that have already been shared. Contact support for help with a shared replay.

Why is an ad unavailable?

An ad may still be loading, there may be no ad available, or a connection or privacy-choice issue may prevent it from loading. Check your connection and try again later. If a privacy message is shown, review your choices. Contact support if the problem continues.

What does Ad-Free remove?

Ad-Free removes gameplay banners and automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose to watch one.

What happens when I reset local progress?

Reset local progress clears game progress on this device. Apple purchases remain restorable. Resetting progress does not remove previously shared replays or Apple purchase records.

Can I play without motion permission?

Yes. Choose Joystick in Settings. Tilt controls use motion permission; the game falls back to joystick controls if motion access is unavailable.
